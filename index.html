Vision & Purpose

About the Conclave

A dedicated two-day platform bringing together industry leaders, academia, researchers, students, and alumni to address evolving skill requirements, contemporary curriculum alignment, and joint research priorities — through keynotes, expert panels, closed-room stakeholder dialogues, technology kiosks, and B2B meetings.

Core Expectations & Pillars

01

Industry Expectations

Identifying concrete core technical competencies, analytical abilities, and leadership skills industry demands from modern maritime graduates.

02

Curriculum Development

Aligning academic programmes, experimental wave tank labs, and capstone design projects directly with maritime industry challenges.

03

Research & Innovation

Engaging in industry-defined problem statements, sponsored R&D initiatives, joint patents, and specialized consulting.

04

B2B Interactions

Facilitating structured one-to-one meetings between maritime enterprises, emerging start-ups, port authorities, and advanced laboratory heads.

05

Institutional Partnerships

Formalizing bilateral Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs), long-term internship pipelines, student exchanges, and joint doctoral supervision.

06

Technology Showcase

Continuous live kiosk demonstrations of underwater robotics, CFD hydrodynamics tools, floating platform models, and prototypes.

Measurable Impact

Target Conclave Outcomes

  • Industry-informed curriculum recommendations tailored for next-generation maritime engineering.
  • Shortlist of industry problem statements & targeted sponsored research themes.
  • Establishment of new MoUs, structured internships, and direct B2B project partnerships.
  • Deepened student-industry engagement, live project exposure, and mentorship channels.
  • Concrete post-event Action Plan with designated faculty-industry coordinators, timelines, and review milestones.
